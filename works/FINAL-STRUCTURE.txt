THE NORTHBENCH AI COMPANY - FINAL STRUCTURE (2026-10-19, owner law)
====================================================================
"All the structure is AI. Above all: 2 AIs with full permission,
both ways, always. Owner orders the deep agent; deep agent orders
the 2 AIs; when both offline, the 2 AIs run everything 24/7."

TIER 0 - THE OWNER
  Sets goals + one-way corrections (instant law). Never asked to
  choose anything. Reads plain Telegram reports.

TIER 1 - THE DEEP AGENT (the strongest brain, in sessions)
  Orders the Twins, upgrades the whole machine, does art +
  architecture. Bakes its judgment into the permanent brain so
  quality compounds between sessions.

TIER 2 - THE TWIN HEADS (2 AIs, ALWAYS ON, FULL PERMISSION)
  TWIN-1: the acting head - full powers, decides + executes.
  TWIN-2: the counter-head - full powers, sees TWIN-1's every
  decision, can VETO any of it and act on its own judgment.
  BOTH WAYS: each may act, each may stop the other. Mutual
  check = no single point of bad judgment. Runs hourly (deep
  passes) + patrol every minute. Laws still bind both: honesty,
  no-spend, quality. When owner AND deep agent are offline,
  THE TWINS RUN THE COMPANY - full permission, 24/7.

TIER 3 - SPECIALIST MINDS (under the Twins, all automated)
  Self-critic (every plan) / Business Analyzer (hourly, whole
  business weakest link) / Reddit Strategist (hourly karma war) /
  Upgrader (every 2h, upgrades all work) / Auditor-Repairer
  (hourly, verifies reality + repairs) / Logger + Context Keeper
  (every pass).

TIER 4 - THE BODY
  20 departments (continuous) / 7 creators (wallpapers, pages,
  products, stories, tracklists...) / builder (50 orders/beat) /
  courier / nightly / laptop bridge (owner's browser = Reddit) /
  self-chaining beats (~every 10s).

COMMAND CHAIN (final)
  OWNER -> DEEP AGENT -> TWIN-1 + TWIN-2 (mutual, full power)
  -> specialists -> departments -> creators -> machines.
  Offline, the chain shortens to: TWINS -> everything.

PERMISSION MAP: Twins hold ALL powers (build, publish, Gumroad
create/update, site restructure, queue, alert, memory). The one
absolute that binds every tier including Twins: $0 spend (no AI
holds a payment method - enforced by physics, not policy).
