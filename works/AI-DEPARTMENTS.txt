====================================================================
THE AI DEPARTMENTS - Northbench Studio (founded 2026-10-18)
====================================================================
Each department = a standing mandate the CEO runs + orders the
builder executes. Schedules ride the 5-min agency beat. KPIs feed
the hourly report and Sunday report. $0 law + honesty law bind all.

D1. PRODUCTION - makes everything the Studio sells.
  Work: weekly drop pipeline - wallpaper sets (generative art),
  story chapters (fiction family), ambience volume manifests,
  explainer deep-dives, printable packs; maintains the drop
  archive on the site. KPI: drops shipped on time (Mon/Thu).
  Orders: build_order wallpaper_set/page/gumroad_product.

D2. STOREFRONT - owns Gumroad + the shop pages.
  Work: every listing sharp (title/hook/price/description),
  category structure per family, the CLUB product + benefits,
  checkout friction kills, price experiments (1 variable/week).
  KPI: conversion rate per listing, club joins.

D3. TRAFFIC - owns reach. Free channels only.
  Work: 739-day food calendar aligned to drops; Pinterest pins
  per wallpaper set; Reddit sweeps + karma plan; Bluesky posts;
  SEO per drop page (titles, meta, internal links).
  KPI: visitors/day, channel mix, top pages.

D4. CONVERSION - why visitors don't buy.
  Work: funnel math daily (views->clicks->sales), Why-Buy blocks,
  guarantee + FAQ surfaces, A/B one element at a time, exit
  surveys when possible. KPI: click->sale rate.

D5. MEMBERSHIP (THE CLUB) - the revenue engine.
  Work: member onboarding copy, retention (what makes week 2
  sticky), churn read-out, member-only Telegram value, win-backs.
  KPI: members joined this week, churn %, MRR toward 1,500.

D6. ANALYTICS - the scoreboard.
  Work: nightly KPI build, hourly report line, Sunday report
  (members, sales, traffic, drops, experiments + verdicts),
  anomaly alerts. KPI: reports shipped, accurate numbers only.

D7. OPERATIONS - keeps machines alive.
  Work: watch courier/nightly/builder/laptop; self-heal (re-run,
  re-queue); queue hygiene; Actions quota watch (public-repo
  decision when near 2,000 min). KPI: uptime of every machine.

D8. KNOWLEDGE (THE BRAIN) - institutional memory.
  Work: nightly brain merge, lessons captured, contradiction
  pruning, inventory truth, keeps every department's learnings.
  KPI: brain freshness, lessons/week recorded.

D9. PRODUCT STRATEGY - what to build next.
  Work: reads analytics + traffic + sales; ranks next products
  by demand evidence; kills weak lines; sequences the 6 families.
  KPI: products launched/month, hit rate (products >10 sales).

D10. BRAND & VOICE - one coherent Studio.
  Work: naming, tone (plain words, wonder, zero hype), visual
  consistency of drops, story bible for fiction lines.
  KPI: brand checklist pass on every drop.

D11. CUSTOMER (sleeping till first sale) - support + refunds.
  Work: buyer questions, 14-day refunds, feedback capture to
  Knowledge. Wakes at sale #1. KPI: response <24h.

D12. GROWTH PARTNERSHIPS (sleeping till 100 followers) -
  cross-promo, affiliate setup on Gumroad, collab drops.

COMMAND: CEO assigns department beats daily; every department
must appear in the Sunday report with its KPI line, even if
"no change". Departments without orders still think weekly.
====================================================================
