====================================================
SUBSCRIPTION RULES (Gumroad
memberships)

Yes-subscription lines: V3
wallpapers, F2 story club, K3 The
Explainer, A2 ambience monthly.
Everything else: one-time.
The law: a subscription opens only
after a one-time product in that
line has real buyers. Earned
recurring, never promised.

====================================================
THE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - the best
way I know to run all this

1. ONE TREE (this doc). The whole
shop, one view, updated when lines
are added.
2. ONE BOARD (PRODUCTION-PLAN.txt).
Every flagship's stage and next
action, updated every session.
3. ONE LOG (GROWTH-CORE/STATE.md).
Every session, every decision,
already running since day one.
4. GATES. Stranger test -> craft
pass -> owner review. Story and
audio always wait for your eyes
and ears. First product of any
new line waits for your review.
5. RHYTHM. The machine markets
tools daily. Each week, one
flagship advances one stage. One
active build per family - never
two, quality cannot split.
6. SUBSCRIPTIONS ARE EARNED. See
the law above.
7. OWNER RESERVED DECISIONS:
series order, story approval,
anything that ships, subscription
launches, account launches.

That is the machine that can carry
six families without one of them
dropping: tree for the map, board
for the week, log for the history,
gates for the bar, rhythm for the
pace.
