3. STRATEGY (the plan)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Funnel: attention (solved: calendar + engines) -> trust (guides,
stories, honest screenshots, guarantees) -> conversion (THE
frontier: $0 sales despite clicks) -> repeat/referral (later).
Current bets, in order: (1) trust surfaces on product pages -
"Why Buy" honesty blocks, guarantee page, real screenshots;
(2) SEO depth on the 9 guides; (3) price-sensitivity testing
within the $3-$50 band; (4) Reddit karma growth to unlock launch
threads; (5) email lane wakes at the $100 payout gate.
North star: first sale, then first 10. Evidence over vibes: every
change gets a KPI read the next night.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HISTORY + LESSONS (what we tried, what it taught)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- Launches on Reddit failed early: karma too low, subreddits
  reject drive-by links. Lesson: earn standing first, launch at
  karma >= 10.
- Clicks exist, sales don't (trust gap, not traffic gap). Lesson:
  the product page must answer "why trust a $0-review shop" with
  real proof, guarantees, and depth - never invented testimonials
  (honesty law: 0 sales = no testimonials exist, ever).
- Free shelf depth (guides/calculators) drives the best traffic
  quality. Lesson: depth converts attention into trust better
  than posts.
- Engines beat willpower: everything that repeats is scheduled.
- Deprecations/cloud quirks (CF models, cron caps, API changes):
  the machine adapts, never buys its way out.
- Pricing: started uniform; moving to value-banded testing.
