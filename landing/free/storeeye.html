Store Eye - a short daily letter about what changed on your shop Shops break quietly. A listing stops being for sale, a price changes to the wrong number, a cover image vanishes, a refund policy line disappears, a link starts pointing nowhere - and nobody notices until a customer does, or worse, nobody ever does. Store Eye is a small Python tool that reads your public pages every day the way a buyer would, then writes you one short letter naming what moved, worst thing first. No dashboard to log into. No noise. Just the letter.
