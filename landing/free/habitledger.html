Habit Ledger - undated 52-week printable planner Habit apps have a design flaw nobody admits: they are built on shame. Miss a day and the app shows you a broken streak, a lost badge, a red mark - as if forgetting once cancels the fifty times you remembered. Habit Ledger is the opposite machine. It is paper, it is undated, and it has no opinion about your Wednesday. You print a sheet, you mark the days you did the thing, and once a month you answer two questions. That is the entire system - and that is why it survives past week three, which is where most habit trackers go to die.
