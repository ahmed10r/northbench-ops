How to tell whether your cold email is working, without guessing

Outreach is a small experiment run on other people's attention. Keep the four numbers that matter and a quiet week becomes information instead of a feeling.

The four numbers

  • Sent: how many emails you actually sent this week, not how many you planned to send.
  • Opened: the count if your tool reports one, treated as roughly right and never as exact.
  • Replied: any reply at all. A no is a reply, and it belongs in the count.
  • Conversations: how many of those replies turned into a real back-and-forth about work.

Read them once a week

  1. Write the four numbers for the week on one line of a plain file.
  2. Put the date at the start of the line. Never edit an old line.
  3. Compare this week with the last three, not with your ambition.
  4. If sends are steady and replies are flat, the message is wrong, not the volume.
  5. If replies are steady but conversations are rare, the offer or the follow-up is where to look.
  6. Change one thing next week. One.

What never to do

  • Do not buy a larger list to fix a message problem. It multiplies the problem.
  • Do not count a send as a success on its own.
  • Do not send the follow-up late; a same-week nudge lands, a three-week one reads like an afterthought.
  • Do not treat someone else's screenshot as your benchmark. Their list, their offer, their timing.

When a reply turns into work

The moment somebody answers, the number that matters changes: whether the price you are about to send covers the work, and whether the terms leave you something at the end. Decide that before the call, not during it.

  • Leave room in the price for the revision round, because there is always one.
  • Keep the same numbers line going after you stop sending. The pattern is what teaches you.

Questions people actually ask

How many emails should I send a week?

Start with a number you can genuinely personalise, and hold it steady for four weeks. A steady small number teaches you something; a large lazy one teaches you nothing.

What reply rate is normal?

Anyone quoting you one is quoting their own list, their own offer and their own country. Yours is the only baseline that will help you, which is exactly why the line is worth keeping.
