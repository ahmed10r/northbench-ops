THE STORY CLUB - WEEK ONE: "The Lighthouse Keeper's Ledger"

The keeper's ledger had two columns: SHIPS PASSED, SHIPS SAVED.

In forty-one years he had recorded 4,212 in the first column.
The second column was empty - until the storm of the 9th, when
he wrote a single entry: "1. The one I was."

They found the ledger on the table, the lamp still turning, and
no keeper anywhere - only wet footprints leading away from the
cliff, in the direction of the town, growing drier as they went,
as if whatever made them had been walking out of the sea for a
very long time and had only just arrived.

The new keeper keeps the ledger now. She has added a third
column. It is unlabeled, and she writes in it every night, and
she will tell you what it counts on the day you take the job
from her.
