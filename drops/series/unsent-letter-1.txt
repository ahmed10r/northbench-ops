UNSENT - LETTER ONE
(to: sara@ / subject: the thing I never mailed / draft 14)

Sara,

I wrote you eleven emails the year you were gone. This is the
first one I will never send, which makes it, I think, the most
honest thing I own.

You left in October. The city did that thing it does when someone
leaves - it kept all your streets and gave them back to me
slightly wrong. I kept your number. Not to call it. Just to know
that somewhere a phone still had you in it.

I am not writing to ask you to come back. I am writing because a
draft is the only place a person can still talk to someone
without being a burden to them. This letter is a room I built
where you are always still on your way home.

Tomorrow, maybe, I will delete this. Or letter twelve.

- d.

[UNSENT continues weekly. Club members receive every letter first.]
